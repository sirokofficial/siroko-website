SRK-001 Recap

A night at sea.

SRK-001 was an amazing first experience for Siroko: a sunset boat party with Barcelona’s international crowd, real connections on deck, and the night ending strong at CDLC.

What's included

  • Access to the SRK-001 Sunset Boat Party
  • 1 drink included per ticket
  • Free access to the official after-party with Zaylune at Carpe Diem / CDLC
  • Experience on one of the biggest boats in Barcelona
  • Siroko photo moments

Details

  • Boarding starts at 17:30

Why it matters

SRK-001 proved the Siroko formula: the right crowd, a premium setting, and a night that continued beyond the boat.
